## Design Specificity Verdict
LLM: authored moments in the student break (strength staircase, emotion-tinted overlay, shared breathing circle, read-aloud, equal "I'd rather not say", fading audio) inside a generic wellness-app shell (white card on beige, teal, pills, emoji illustrations). Teacher side is interchangeable with any admin dashboard; the self-advocacy metric (self-requested 36% → 50%) is buried. Nunito declared but never loaded; ⏸ renders as a broken glyph.
Deterministic: CLI 4 warnings (cream-palette x3, low-contrast muted 4.0:1 on index.html); CLI cannot see JS-rendered screens (src scan [] = coverage gap). Browser (7 states): pervasive low-contrast (white on teal 3.85, muted on cream 4.0, teal pills 3.2, gray pills 3.7) — true positive, both assessments agree; side-tab stripes on 5 .freq-rows (agrees with LLM); nested-cards (.prompt in student card); cream-palette (taste rule, deliberate low-stimulation choice). Missed by detector: ▶ preview contrast 3.4, sub-44px stimulus/🔊 targets, unlabeled textarea#reason and select#who, focus escapes break overlay. No overflow at 390/820.
