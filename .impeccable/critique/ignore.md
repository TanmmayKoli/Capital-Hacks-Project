# Accepted findings
- cream-palette: the warm off-white background (#f5f3ee) is a deliberate low-glare, sensory-friendly choice for autistic students. It's documented in DESIGN.md.
- design-system-color (emotion tints in src/content.js, chart colors in src/charts.js): each emotion carries color/ink/soft values by design. These are the only colors allowed inline.
